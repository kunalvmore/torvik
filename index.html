3D coach

Watch every move before you try it

All 20 exercises have a 3D coach that shows the full movement, slows down the hard part and lights up the muscles that are working.

  • A timeline of every phase of the rep, with a cue for each
  • Switch camera angles and slow it down to half speed
  • Muscles worked and the mistakes to avoid
Camera form check

Your camera counts reps and checks your form

Prop your iPhone up and train. On 15 exercises the front camera counts your reps, scores your form and shows the one thing to fix.

  • Video is analysed on your iPhone and never saved or uploaded
  • Tap − or + if it misses a rep
  • The 3D demo is one tap away
Plans

Know exactly what to do today

Pick a goal and the equipment you have. Follow a ready-made plan, or build your own workout. Torvik guides every set with a rest timer and voice cues.

  • Four plans: First Steps, Dumbbell Strength, Bodyweight Basics, Core & Posture
  • See which day is next and how far you’ve come
  • Build your own workouts in seconds
Progress

Watch yourself get stronger

Every set is logged for you. See your weekly goal fill up, your streak grow and which muscles still need their turn.

  • Weekly goal, time trained and streak
  • Sets per muscle group, so nothing gets skipped
  • Personal records and strength over time
