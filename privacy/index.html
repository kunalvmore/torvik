Privacy policy

Your training. Your data. Nobody else’s.

Last updated 24 November 2026. Torvik has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Here’s exactly what the app stores, and what it never does.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up and no ads.
  • No analytics or tracking. We don’t collect any data from you.
  • The camera is used only on your iPhone. Video is never saved or uploaded.
  • Your workouts are stored on your iPhone and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own iCloud account.
  • You can export or delete your workout data at any time.

Who we are

Torvik is an iPhone app for strength training. In this policy “we” means Torvik. You can reach us at mkunal065@gmail.com.

What Torvik stores

Torvik stores the information you create in the app: your workouts and sets (exercise, weight, reps, hold times and form scores), workouts you build, your progress in a plan, and your settings such as units, weekly goal and reminder times. This information is stored on your iPhone.

The camera

Camera form check uses the front camera to count your reps and check your form. The video is analysed on your iPhone as you train. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Only the result, such as the rep count and form score, is kept with your workout. You can turn off camera access at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync in Torvik’s Settings, your workout data is stored in your private iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit, so it can sync across your devices. We can’t see or access this data. Apple’s privacy policy covers how iCloud stores it. You can turn iCloud sync off at any time.

Notifications

If you turn on workout reminders, Torvik schedules them on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server and we don’t receive any information about them.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t collect, sell or share personal information.
  • We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don’t ask for your name, email address, location or health data.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking, and its fonts are served from this site. If you use the “Notify me” form, your email address is used only to tell you when a new version is out.

Exporting and deleting your data

In Settings, “Export workouts” creates a spreadsheet file (CSV) of your sets that you can save or share where you choose. “Delete workout data” removes your history, workouts, plan progress and preferences from your iPhone, and optionally from iCloud. Deleting the app also removes the data stored on your iPhone.

Children

Torvik is a general fitness app and is not directed at children under 13. Because we don’t collect any personal information, we don’t knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example when a future version adds subscriptions, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

Contact
