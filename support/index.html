Stuck on something? We’ve got your back.

Quick answers about form check, syncing and your data, so you can get back to training.

How does camera form check work?

Open an exercise with the camera mark and tap Form check. Prop your iPhone up so your whole body is in view, about 2 metres away. The camera counts reps, scores your form and shows the one thing to fix. If it misses a rep, tap − or +.

Which exercises have form check?

15 of the 20 exercises: goblet squat, reverse lunge, split squat, Romanian deadlift, glute bridge, push-up, incline push-up, dumbbell bench press, shoulder press, lateral raise, triceps kickback, single-arm row, bent-over row, biceps curl and plank. They have a camera mark in the Exercises tab.

Is my camera video saved or uploaded?

No. The video is analysed on your iPhone while you train and is never recorded or sent anywhere. Read the privacy policy for details.

How do I sync between my devices or move to a new iPhone?

Turn on iCloud sync in Settings › Your data on each device, signed in with the same Apple Account. Your workouts and plan progress sync through your private iCloud.

How do I export my workouts?

Go to Settings › Export workouts, choose the date range and whether to include form scores, then save the CSV file to Files or share it. It opens in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets.

How do I start over?

Go to Settings › Delete workout data. You can save a copy first, and choose whether to also delete from iCloud. This can’t be undone.

My workout reminders don’t show up.

Check that reminders are on in Torvik’s Settings, and that notifications are allowed for Torvik in the iPhone Settings app under Notifications.

Does Torvik cost anything?

No. Torvik is free, with every feature included. There are no ads and no account.
